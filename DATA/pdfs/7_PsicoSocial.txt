DIESAFE
We are here for planning our last momments before death at least

WHY
Feel OK when imagining your moment of death
After someone dies, there are a lot of worries that are hard to manage for the relatives if the dead
person hasn't specified them, specially when the event comes as a surprise.
We do Testaments but we could detail them more. For example: with scheduled actions after
reaching certain thresholds, and so on... .
There is much wisdom to retain about our relationship with the moment of death from «The Tibetan
Book of Life and Death». But the Tibetan culture nor the platforms mentioned above haven't
developed any easy interactive tool for guiding you to declare and well specify a settled plan for
your moment of death, that could either help you for your having more calm about it andor for your
plan inspiring others.
There was an active community called digitallegacyconference.com and non-free applications like
safebeyond, ether9, or deadsocial (in development, but with a lot of documentation), that basically
specialize in keeping your data online after you die. One catalan communitty did a step forward and
created a communitty service called somprovisionals that deal with some part of the funeral services
with a further cooperative-ethico-economical point of view (there are many minimonopolies at
funeral services). There is also a trend called Death cafes, where people gather to share thoughts
about their own death while relaxly taking a coffee.


WHAT
Tidy your thoughts about how you want to experience the last moments of your life and what would
you like happen after you will die.
Plan your companionship and environtment premortem and your postmortem heritage.
Describe not only a possible environment desired for your death, but several of them.
Schedule many possibly postmortem actions.



HOW
DieSafe as FUZZY (Advanced) Death App.
Do a test and never worry about taking such plannings for granted. Don't do It if - When - while you
don't feel so. Publish contradictory plans if you want it too, you can't really know how you will feel
by then, so never worry too much about It happening perfectly by then as you predesigned it before
through the app.
Plan some triggers to do certain actions at a whatever post-mortem time to keep your legacy more
assured or just for fun (i.e. i invite a beer to x members after every summer rain, etc.)

SOULS
Your masks are more real than what you say

WHY
Information resuming you is the more important information you could know and share. You could
resume your Profile/About you with 2 or 3 keywords.
Keywords are sometimes difficult to be expressed or found in a direct-formal way. Making it easily,
pleasantly and meaningfully doable is the more challenging feature for a platform to specify how to
fill it (and encourage users to do so).
Users normally (poorly) fill their About you descritpion field up because they consider it too
reductive andor over exposes them. Declaring your main values isn't easy most of the times, and our
decalaration of them are subjected not only to possible future changes, but more specially to the
intended audience we direct them to.
Souls app proposes you to describe many About you(s) by masking yourself with-as some other
fictional entity. This is not proposing anything new, this is called "projective test" technique within
psychology.
"Mask" is just a nicer way to call to a Perspective. Our truths are just perspectives, and we can have
many for describing-giving truth to a same thing.
Everyone of us, at some point, will try trolling the projective test thinking that we are giving fake or
overbroad misguiding replies to it (neutral or funny adjectives instead of more qualificative ones)
but there is nothing alien to us in whatever we estate. The more effort put in hiding them, the more
advanced topology we propose with them, the hardest task to achieve for analisers, in more formal
terms.
This strategical hiding, more in fact and a bit paradoxically, just points to a deeper symbolism that
those (apparently meaningless) keywords have for him-her because there is nothing alien in us, and
perhaps you were destined to say (and see yourself with) so.


WHAT
Souls is a projected app for people expressing their keywords through projective ways.
Within projectivities, there are some of them more useful than others for specifics data minings. We
propose the default Animal & Colour tests-masks-projections are the simpler closer and broad
symbolisms. Doing it within animals helps us recapitulate our ontogeny. The better we feed our
deep memories, the better we will predict anything thereafter as well.

ADORE YOU
Adore you, a trustful projected app
Formally ask for interacting closely with someone else
WHY: We are bound to bond with other people. It is very natural and lovely to want to bond with
someone by sensing his-her body (andor even just his/her mind...) with full consent, but sometimes
such wants are either difficult to express andor are proposed in a too uncomfortable way andor not
enough transparently to the receiver. This AdoreYou app wishes to help you tunning these kind of
requests. "Adore you" as a name because it emphasizes the best of the wills at trying to contact
someone.
AdoreYou purpose is to, at the very least, cheer up the relation and allow for a more relaxed
conversation about such wants, if the receiver have liked the request. At most, just help people who
want to fulfill their wants of adoring someone else openly in many ways, just for a momment or
more regularly.
We shouldn't value more the fear of damaging some relationship by being open about what we wish
than the potential benefits that doing so can give to the relation. Wills and wants of adoring others
are anyway going to be more informally expressed, they risking being either pains within ourselves
due to keep that conversation within ourselves only andor by giving disgusting communication sent
to the adored one.
In the case of adoring requests being accepted, it can cause a very lovely impact in the relationship.
The possibility of using the app for harassment is minimized by giving all privacies the permissions
to the users for being contacted by who they want and through which kind of messages only.
Possible harassment is just not minimized but also further tackled. Receivers will have additional
proof of not consenting being contacted by who they don't want andor they have refused them in the
app.
AdoreYou also opens up a very interesting negotiating arena, where receivers can refuse requesters
partially instead of having to do it absolutly, even asking them back for bidding higher for their
acceptance. I.e. I additionally want to know your ID, have a videocall, etc

OWNTRUST / EVIL CODE

      You to trust first who you would like to trust you

WHY
You could request someone to trust by offering some material grants to him/her. But beyond
compensatory material grants, you can also grant him/her to cause you inmaterial damages in case
you don’t comply
Such compensation won’t give back the material value (s)he lost, but could make him-her
convinced to trust you more, because (s)he could see that for you, (s)he being openly able to
damage your reputation is more important for you than losing any materiality you granted him/her
with.
This crumbling will informally happen anyway if there is a trouble, with the risks of not calculating
well the proportionality of the counterattack, either being too little or too much. For example:
Negative comments on profiles are a popular way for easily building trust in a network, but if you
explicitly (and not only implicitly like how it is designed in the platforms…) allow others to
comment negatively in your profile, there is a further added value to the negative comment, while
avoids problems of discomfort or brusqueness (…) both for the one who gives the negative
comment and for the one who receives it.


WHAT
Equalize risks and feel a more fair relation by explicitly (beyond offering them material grants)
consenting others you want them to trust you, to explicitly be able to damage your reputation to the
extent you choose. i.e.
«I allow you to get my bike, insult me and tell my groups and friends and put slanderous posters
down the street, but only until a month after I have not returned it to you your motorbike as we
agreed»
«I don’t know you and you ask me for staying in my house and I have to trust you… Well… So to
have a more fairer relationship you should trust me too! in allowing me to damage your reputation
if you do not comply with what we agreed to?… It’s not fair if you do not let me to maybe hurt you
while i have to maybe let you hurt me»
«Your cousin (user1) also offers you an OwnTrust if you need it…»

Todas las materias y especies tenemos algunos momentos en los que nos juntamos por
algún motivo porque nos beneficia a su supervivencia personal y como grupo: aparearse,
para conseguir comida, para construir una casa mejor, para emigrar o para la mas simple
proteccion ante depredadores.

En esos encuentros construimos mecanismos que automaticen la realizacion colectiva de
tareas por la superviviencia comun.
Ese ente comun donde las especies nos encontramos permanentemente tiene dos caras.
Una informal cultural y una formal social.

Aparte de los encuentros abstractos en lo cultural y lo social, seguimos desarrollando
encuentros presenciales puntuales donde tendemos a querer mostrar minimizadas a las
necesidades de superviviencia mas practicas identificandolas con otras necesidades mas
abstractas, las que si nos esforzamos en desarrollar en tales encuentros presenciales.

Mas creativa una juntanza es cuando mas le quitamos la parte de presión práctica que
tenemos para juntarnos.

Una manera facil para magnificar el hecho mismo de encontrarse presencialmente es
adornar el espacio mismo donde nos juntamos Junto a la “sacralización” del espacio,
viene consigo la sacralización de las relaciones que se llevan a cabo ahí, y o viceversa.

Para alguien conseguir que se sigan sus ciertos metodos para su rito durante el
explicitado encuentro, ha de encontrar cuestiones que trasciendan la superviviencia mas
practica pero que tambien se refieran a ella, y presentarlas minimizadas y suficientemente
sutiles tambien como vehiculo de cohesion cultural y social.

Muchos animales están mucho mas agusto con sus soledades que nosotras,
seguramente sea porqué no tienen tanta noción del enigma que hay en el entorno como
nosotros. Un perro mira a las estrellas y no se imagina un sol lejano. Pero en cambio a
nosotros si nos toca pasar por eso trago de potencial terror de vernos tan solos en tan
gran espacio. Nosotros, cuando mas solos nos intentamos llegar a sentir, no vamos a
poder dejar de estar acompañados por nuestra propia sensacion - pulsion de (futura)
muerte, donde podemos vernos estar relacionandonos desde un maximo inframundo
omnipresente hasta con solo con nuestro mas minimo y simple momento premortem.

La muerte es el objeto con que mas profunda y facilmente reflexionamos sobre nuestra
relacionalidad con todo lo demas, por eso es una herramienta tan popular de cohesion
cultural y social.

junto a sus antepasados en un sitio (cementerio) concreto. Tambien a nivel humanoide

parte fundamental de los fundamentos de la ley positiva de hoy en dia.

Pero ¿Como vamos a diferenciar a quien nos ofrece mejor conectividad con lo pasado ya
muerto para que los demas sigamos su proposición de rito?

Puede que pensemos que con los muertos todos podemos conectar por igual, pero quien

claridad lo que a los demas nos hace dudar tanto, por eso puede que convenza a los
demas para seguir su rito durante los encuentros presenciales.

En la humanada, una de las formas especiálmente facil y agradecido de acercarse a la
relacion tanto con nuestro inicio de vida (zygoto 1-2) como con la muerte (1-0), es
mediante el culto de los sueños y con las sobredosis de “plantas visionarias” en estados
de vigilia, ambos laboratorios de la creatividad humana desde tiempos inmemoriables, y
que muy bien podrian ser la causa tanto de nuestro salto especial a humanoides hace
200.000 años como de los muchos conocimientos avanzados emergidos que contrarian a
la vision de "troglodita incapaz con tantos enlaces perdidos" que tenemos de y con
nuestros antepasados.

Domar a lo muerto le da una autoridad social a quien demuestra que lo consigue mejor.
En este sentido, si usamos a animales como totems navegamos mas ancestral y
suavemente a nuestros propios muertos. Animales amistados varios que son muestra y

En este sentido, se puede desarrolar el arte de la comunicación avanzada con animales

SOCIOLOGIA EVOLUTIVA: Celebración reivindicativa
Cada especie empezamos a valorar el juntarnos con otros semejantes cuando vemos que
compartimos la manera de afrontar y resolver nuestra incertidumbre.
Todas las materias y especies tenemos algunos momentos en los que nos juntamos por
algún motivo porque nos beneficia a su supervivencia personal y como grupo: aparearse,
para conseguir comida, para construir una casa mejor, para emigrar o para la mas simple
proteccion ante depredadores.
En esos encuentros construimos mecanismos que automaticen la realizacion colectiva de
tareas por la superviviencia comun.
Ese ente comun donde las especies nos encontramos permanentemente tiene dos caras.
Una informal cultural y una formal social.
Aparte de los encuentros abstractos en lo cultural y lo social, seguimos desarrollando
encuentros presenciales puntuales donde tendemos a querer mostrar minimizadas a las
necesidades de superviviencia mas practicas identificandolas con otras necesidades mas
abstractas, las que si nos esforzamos en desarrollar en tales encuentros presenciales.
Mas creativa una juntanza es cuando mas le quitamos la parte de presión práctica que
tenemos para juntarnos.
Una manera facil para magnificar el hecho mismo de encontrarse presencialmente es
adornar el espacio mismo donde nos juntamos Junto a la “sacralización” del espacio,
viene consigo la sacralización de las relaciones que se llevan a cabo ahí, y o viceversa.
Para alguien conseguir que se sigan sus ciertos metodos para su rito durante el
explicitado encuentro, ha de encontrar cuestiones que trasciendan la superviviencia mas
practica pero que tambien se refieran a ella, y presentarlas minimizadas y suficientemente
sutiles tambien como vehiculo de cohesion cultural y social.
Muchos animales están mucho mas agusto con sus soledades que nosotras,
seguramente sea porqué no tienen tanta noción del enigma que hay en el entorno como
nosotros. Un perro mira a las estrellas y no se imagina un sol lejano. Pero en cambio a
nosotros si nos toca pasar por eso trago de potencial terror de vernos tan solos en tan
gran espacio. Nosotros, cuando mas solos nos intentamos llegar a sentir, no vamos a
poder dejar de estar acompañados por nuestra propia sensacion - pulsion de (futura)
muerte, donde podemos vernos estar relacionandonos desde un maximo inframundo
omnipresente hasta con solo con nuestro mas minimo y simple momento premortem.
La muerte es el objeto con que mas profunda y facilmente reflexionamos sobre nuestra
relacionalidad con todo lo demas, por eso es una herramienta tan popular de cohesion
cultural y social.
La ritualizacion del hecho de morir lo evidencian los elefantes que buscan irse a morir
junto a sus antepasados en un sitio (cementerio) concreto. Tambien a nivel humanoide
tenemos a los dolmenes de antepasados nuestros y a la necromancia juridica que es
parte fundamental de los fundamentos de la ley positiva de hoy en dia.
Pero ¿Como vamos a diferenciar a quien nos ofrece mejor conectividad con lo pasado ya
muerto para que los demas sigamos su proposición de rito?
Puede que pensemos que con los muertos todos podemos conectar por igual, pero quien
se acerca mas a la experiecia de la muerte, mas puede decir que ha visto con mas
claridad lo que a los demas nos hace dudar tanto, por eso puede que convenza a los
demas para seguir su rito durante los encuentros presenciales.
En la humanada, una de las formas especiálmente facil y agradecido de acercarse a la
relacion tanto con nuestro inicio de vida (zygoto 1-2) como con la muerte (1-0), es
mediante el culto de los sueños y con las sobredosis de “plantas visionarias” en estados
de vigilia, ambos laboratorios de la creatividad humana desde tiempos inmemoriables, y
que muy bien podrian ser la causa tanto de nuestro salto especial a humanoides hace
200.000 años como de los muchos conocimientos avanzados emergidos que contrarian a
la vision de "troglodita incapaz con tantos enlaces perdidos" que tenemos de y con
nuestros antepasados.
Domar a lo muerto le da una autoridad social a quien demuestra que lo consigue mejor.
En este sentido, si usamos a animales como totems navegamos mas ancestral y
suavemente a nuestros propios muertos. Animales amistados varios que son muestra y
prueba de una nueva comunión y creencia de que somos una parte avanzada de entre
todos ellos, y no solo chimpancés avanzados… .
En este sentido, se puede desarrolar el arte de la comunicación avanzada con animales
donde va a ganar mas puntos quien consiga traer consigo al lugar de reunion presencial a
bestias mas salvajes, ancestros - muertos mas antiguos que los de nuestra propia
especie, ya sea como totems o hasta incluso como domesticados.
En todo encuentro presencial ritualizado hay una reivindicación implicita que empieza
como una autoreivindicación propositiva ante “los dioses” del hecho de que “estamos
vivos”. Nuestra celebración reivindicativa por la vida, por la cohesión social de nuestros
clanes y especie, magnificados con cultos a nuestros ancestros muertos y animales los
queremos retener y no desplazarlos solo a espacios especializados. Así lo muestran las
primeras casas de afuera de las cuevas (Catalohyuk) donde había que arrastrarse y
entrar por puertas de menos de un metro para pasar de una habitación a otra (por
mantener el enlace con la vida en la cueva...), y donde mas especialmente, aún había
figuras de toros y pechos y genitales femeninos salientes de las paredes de la “iglesia -
sala de estar”.
La soledad misma nuestra es un autovictimismo con el que reivindicamos mejoras para
nuestra vida y que no vamos a poder redimir del todo nunca. Todos estamos solos ante
nuestra propia muerte, nadie puede morir por otro.
También en toda relacion y mas especialemente en este tipo de relaciones tan
aglutinantes que proponen la simbologia que dirige el encuentro, hay una discriminación y
a quien se le discrimina ya se le puede considerar como víctima.
Ambos privilegiados y discriminados desarrollaran un victimismo para justificar sus
reinvindicaciones practicas. El victimismo tambien tiene sus limites. Lo privilegiado habrá
de abstraer mas su victimismo a mas privilegios practicos tenga y quiera tener,
dificultandose la labor y entrando mas en el riesgo del karma propio y la posibilidad de
conflicto con otros. Lo discriminado tendrá mas facil justificar su victimismo, al que habrá
de complementar con soluciones propositivas practicas que puedan llevarle a dejar de
estar discriminado (no necesitar tanta victimizacion para el encuentro).
Al crecer las discriminaciones va a haber mas victimas que menos, estas van a querer
usar el hecho de las juntanzas celebratorias para conspirar entre ellas sobre como dejar
de sufrir discriminación. Sus espacios y momentos de encuentro tenderan a tener mucho
volumen victimista donde poder regocijarse y aun mas necesidad de operar
diligentemente para superar su discriminacion. Donde por estos dos elementos
combinados, pueden llegar a usar sus espacios como momentos terapeuticos
cohesionantes solamente, y en la practica acomodarse de algun modo en su papel de
discriminado, por los pequeños privilegios del facil jugo que el victimismo le ofrece
comparado con la mucho mas ardua labor que le es el desarrollar sus propias
alternativas.
Hoy en dia, mas alla de las juntanzas que genera la burocracia social y economica, hay
muchas celebraciones lúdico-festivas paganas, religiosas y culturales. Por otro lado hay
manifestaciones de protesta, huelgas, incluso llamadas al boycot. Aparte tenemos una
herramienta (global-mind.org) que desde algunos años está intentando medir como
afectan al entorno las congregaciones con el fin de demostrar que en los picos de
intención compartida es donde mas se manifiesta nuestra capacidad de influencia fisica
en el entorno.
El reto queda sugerido pues: Demostremos que vamos a por el mejor pico de intención
conjunto que puede haber. Cultivemos para el, el balance entre la reivindicación
confrontativa y el ambiente ludico que propongamos a la hora de juntarnos. El ejemplo
concreto mas retante para ello que se me ocurre es la colectivización de celebracion
reivindicativa del 8 marzo, pero tal vez no nos atrevamos a tanto, y prefiramos atrevernos
con muchas otras de reivindicar celebrando lo vivos que estamos.

Thematic SECTIONS

Develop thematic interest for the commons

Ex: Flove.org/housing
Do something personal with social impact in the locality where is the house that welcomes you

Visitors welcomed in the houses of the network, in exchange for the stay, participate in tasks that
generate a social impact in the town or city where the house is (besides the contributions to the
houses themselves).


Other active hospitality networks do not have such reach. There are global hospitality networks
such as couchsurfing, or the more politically minded like trustroots (activism) or servas (peace).
There are also regional networks such as the Iberian Ecovillage Network, or activist networks
related to urban social centers, but workaway.info and woofers are the best examples of formal
networks to be improved.


Woofers have a filter for the projects to be related to organic agriculture, and their adverts are quite
reliable, but every host pays 20 or more per year of being published there, and it doesn't involve
other areas of action besides organic agriculture. Workaway.info does involve broader areas of
action and contributions given, so it is a better network to be improved. None of them specify a
maximum contributing time owners can ask for, so they may be unfair for the guests.


This section want to simplify volunteering, foster more collaboration between this type of projects
so that the there is more contribution to them. A network of houses organized with a desire for
external impact to the house, better prevents some unsocial behavior from the guest and or owners,
and so both parties feel that this house is not the only one where the guest can be. It is always
better for the guest to go to another house before creating trouble in the one (s)he is at the moment.
Also the fact that a guest contributes to the development of a common project that goes beyond the
improvement of the house itself, helps improving the coliving by establishing a common
referential outer purpose, therefore it transcends the house relationship vices between owners and
volunteers, thus prevents potential problems from being experienced or personalized.


The interested purpose for the network itself is to help as a common trustee for both parties and
give the best recommendations for guests routes and tasks. By matching where would the guest
better fit, the guest is more calm and happy in his-her daily life so that (s)he can devote his-her
talent to develop social projects, where this section of shared residences would be an obvious
candidate as a task itself.


More sustainable and pleasant co-livings
On the one hand, we can help many people who are willing to do things they do not know where to
start or are ashamed to contact the projects and need a hostess to accompany them at the
beginning. There are also activists who do not have a stable house, want to move and there are
many people who are simply looking to live for a while in environments where everything is more
shared. On the other hand, there are many other people who already have a house, they would like
to share it more and also they would like that to leave a good mark on their environment, but they
have trouble finding the right people to make it happen.


We are aware of the problems of co-living that are generated when interacting in homes, but we
are also aware that many of them could be avoided with a better specification of the conditions of
use of the house and more specifically, with the house being part of a network of houses with
social impact where there is some other houses (and spirits!) watching out there.


This is how you get the guest to have the permanent research to know if this is the best house
where to develop your skills for an improvement of the house and society, while also encouraging
the friendliness of the landlady to want the guest choose to be in his house and not in another.


Networks mediation tools
We like the simple life, but we also have offer to formalize any relationship with some type of
contract and guarantee, if the owners or the volunteers so wish.


Some owners may want additional guarantees about the guests. Some of them may want
guarantees that the guest accepts an authority of an agent on behalf of both, so it can ask him-her
to go somewhere else without the owner having to bother to tell him-her so. There can be many
decentralized types of doing this backing, but it is also possible and wished to automate it (make it
completely decentralized, only based in relational data).


Standard for shared residency offers
We specify this standard of residence offer where other possible exchanges related to the residence
are proposed, but also limited. We do it to be transparent with our ideals, and so, if an owner wants
to say «this house is shared» (without more conditions or without belonging to the network of
residences), that the applicant knows what he is offering in particular .


Our proposal is that without counting as beyond the hours contributed to the needed for the
feeding, cleaning or basic maintenance of the space, the maximum request that the owners should
offer to the guests would be:


2 hours = Sleep and basic breakfast

3 hours = Sleep, breakfast and lunch

4 hours = Complete diet
In a more formed network, we would like to be the ones who would supplement the diets and even
small rents to the voluntary guests.

THE NON INTEGRAL FLAW
Free Software for a Free society is a famous book from R. Stallman, but the FreeSociety
standard wasn’t much evolved there. Free software scope is only about the non scarce
products of Software and Documentation. Not even a more Free documentation for a Free
Society was evolved within Free Software communitty, hence the Creative Commons
appeared as a spin off from that.
The Creative Commons and GNU&FSF had the same comfort of their aimed licenses for
digital products not having to deal with scarcity. It is not ilegal to use Free Software and or
Free Culture licenses to scarce physical products, but it’s not a common practice at all.
Even, at some articles, R. Stallman contradicts his will of a Freer society by justifying that
is somewhat impossible to apply the freedom licensing trends from software to more
scarce products. CC neither want bringing their Sharing practices to more physical and
scarce products.
Shareful licensing challenges these thoughts by offering a set of much lighter (more easily
usable) standard clauses appliable to scarce products. But not even the including of a
Shareful license to a Hardware used for creating Free Culture through Free Software will
be enough Freedom for society, it will be just one more addon for it(s uthopic way).
Specifically, shareful, is beyond the scope of this FreeData standard, it was only
meantioned for a better understanding of the freedata frame and relations with outer closer
initiatives.
Either at Open and or Free standards initiatives, there is the learnt knowledge and flaw
that the standard doesn’t grant much freedom overall because just focus in a part of the
product, that have other needed parts for operating it that are not that much free, so taking
most or much of the intended freedom away anyway.
So we better think on proposing a bigger scope than the ones we already have where all
of them can be more easily seen in relation with each other, while not needing to
integralize it completely to all the scopes of life here. We can rather assume limits more
openly and with less stress for perfection and reference to the outer projects that are
pursuing freedom in the more related ways and complementary scopes of the FreeData
one.

What good and not enough at other related projects
mydata.org
This FreeData standard is completely aligned with mydata petition. FreeData adds on the
top of it extensions and improvements in the form of a more clear definition of categories
and depth of the standard.
Mydata is a communitty that proposes to share some principles for data management
resumed in a petition (in a manifesto like style). Some key points at it propose very
ambituous objectives with very open-to-interpretation words style (i.e. portability, control,
my, etc). This is intended for gathering support from a broad audience. The petition also
manifests its willing to support the ongoing projects which comply with the standards and
proposes the development of new prototypes for non-existing practices that are anyway
proposed in its standard. But there comes the flaw: More concrete details about the
methodology for support or develop prototypes is not further specified, hence such
ambitious proposals are left without further mechanics to moderate contributions for
practically (really) implementing the declared intentions from the standard. Their
broadness format is an strategic aesthetic-move for the project to serve as a
communitarian umbrella for cross-colaboration between separated projects with affinity to
the values expressed there, but not for any concrete common project development beyond
the organizing of one annual gathering and related bits.
Since mydata appeared as a need to develop collaborative ways to manage data in a
historical moment where access to the resource was not enough decentralized, mydata
does already enough by being only a communitarian umbrella (a place for low moderated
projects cross-collaboration), and don’t need to propose more concrete standards (either
philosophically or technically-really), nor get to develop altogether some key prototypes.
By being just an umbrella it does better its communitary function with its low moderated
entry and enforcements.
Mydata could do a step forward and develop metrics appliable to projects, but mydata
could also perfectly don’t develop them since there could be some very participant projects
in the umbrella which would score a low compliance of such standards, and by being more
clearly pointed as not much communitarian, that could discourage its participation (i.e.
such projects marketing would recommend to ‘not link that much to a cluster where they
show other competing projects as better than ours’).
We can love mydata as an umbrella but we should mechanize, either in and outside it,
further the wished features explained in mydata petition.
